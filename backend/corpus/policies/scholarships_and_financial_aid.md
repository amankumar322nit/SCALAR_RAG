# Scaler Scholarships and Financial Aid Options

## 1. Women in Tech Scholarship
- Scaler provides a dedicated Women in Tech scholarship of up to ₹25,000 off tuition fees for eligible female candidates aiming to upskill into software engineering or data science roles.
- Eligibility: Submission of a short statement of purpose (SOP) and scoring above the 75th percentile in the Scaler National Scholarship Test (NST).

## 2. Merit-Based Scholarships
- **Top Performers**: Learners who achieve top 5% rank in the entrance assessment receive merit scholarships ranging from ₹15,000 to ₹35,000.
- Scholarships are credited directly against the final course fee invoice and cannot be redeemed for cash.

## 3. Financing & EMI Plans
- **No-Cost EMI Options**: Scaler partners with leading financial institutions and NBFCs (including Propelld, LiquiLoans, Eduvanz, and HDFC Bank) to provide 0% interest EMI tenures of 6, 9, and 12 months.
- **Extended EMI Plans**: Low-interest EMI plans are available for up to 24 and 36 months for working professionals seeking flexible monthly commitments.
- **Documentation Requirements for Loan Approval**: Government ID (Aadhaar / Passport), PAN Card, and latest 3 months' bank statements / salary slips.
