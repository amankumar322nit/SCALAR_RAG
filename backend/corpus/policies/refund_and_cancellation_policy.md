# Scaler Refund and Cancellation Policy

## 1. 7-Day Unconditional Money-Back Guarantee
Scaler offers a 7-day unconditional money-back guarantee for all newly enrolled learners in Scaler Academy and Scaler Data Science & Machine Learning programs.
- The 7-day period begins on the official batch commencement date (Day 1 of live classes).
- If a learner submits a withdrawal request via the Learner Dashboard or by emailing support@scaler.com within these 7 calendar days, a 100% full refund of the tuition fee paid will be issued.
- No cancellation charges or administrative fees are deducted during the 7-day cooling-off window.

## 2. Pro-Rata Refund Policy (Days 8 to 30)
If a learner requests cancellation between Day 8 and Day 30 following batch commencement:
- A pro-rata refund is calculated based on the completed module percentage.
- An administrative processing fee of ₹10,000 (or equivalent in foreign currency) will be deducted.
- Any non-refundable registration/seat reservation deposit (up to ₹15,000) is excluded from the refund.

## 3. Post 30-Day Policy (No Refunds)
- After 30 calendar days from the batch start date, no refunds (full or partial) will be entertained under any circumstances.
- Learners facing medical emergencies or unforeseen personal hardships may instead apply for a "Batch Pause / Batch Deferral" of up to 6 months without forfeiting their tuition fee.

## 4. Refund Processing Timeline and Modes
- Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment source (Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, or UPI) within 7 to 10 working business days.
- In cases where the course was financed through a third-party NBFC loan partner (e.g., Propelld, LiquiLoans), the refund is processed directly to the lending partner to close or adjust the loan account.
