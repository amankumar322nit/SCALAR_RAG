# Scaler Placement Assistance and Career Support Policy

## 1. Eligibility Criteria for Placement Support
Learners become eligible for placement support through the Scaler Career Portal upon fulfilling the following requirements:
- Minimum 85% attendance across all live classes and mentor sessions.
- Completion of at least 80% of mandatory homework assignments and coding contests.
- Clearing the Pre-Placement Readiness Assessment (PPRA) with a minimum score of 70%.
- Completion of at least 2 full mock technical interviews and 1 HR mock interview with an assigned industry mentor.

## 2. Career Support Services Provided
- **Dedicated Career Coach**: Every eligible learner is assigned a career coach who assists in resume crafting, LinkedIn profile optimization, and portfolio review.
- **Mock Interviews**: Access to 1-on-1 mock interviews conducted by senior engineers working at top tech firms (FAANG, Tier-1 startups, unicorns).
- **Scaler Hiring Partner Network**: Access to exclusive job opportunities across Scaler's network of 900+ partner hiring companies including Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Uber, Flipkart, Swiggy, and Razorpay.
- **Salary Negotiation & Offer Evaluation**: Guidance on evaluating multiple offers and negotiating competitive compensation packages.

## 3. Placement Support Window and Validity
- Placement assistance remains active for up to 12 months after the official course graduation date.
- Learners who accept a job offer through the Scaler network or an off-campus referral while enrolled remain part of the alumni network but exit the active placement pool.

## 4. Fair Practices and Code of Conduct
- Learners must accept or decline an offer within 48 hours of receiving the formal offer letter.
- Renege policy: Accepting an offer and subsequently backing out without a valid medical or documented emergency results in immediate revocation of placement portal access.
