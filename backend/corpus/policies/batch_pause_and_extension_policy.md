# Scaler Batch Pause and Course Extension Policy

## 1. Batch Pause Guidelines
- Learners enrolled in Scaler Academy or Scaler Data Science can apply for a formal Batch Pause if they face work pressure, examinations, medical emergencies, or personal commitments.
- **Maximum Pause Duration**: A learner is entitled to pause their batch for a cumulative maximum period of 6 months throughout the course duration.
- **Eligibility to Pause**: Learners must have completed at least the first introductory module (Module 1) and have no pending fee dues.

## 2. Procedure to Request a Pause
- Submit a request through the Learner Dashboard under "Course Settings > Request Batch Pause" at least 7 days before the start of the next module.
- In case of sudden medical emergencies, submit the doctor's certificate within 5 days of absence.
- Upon resumption, the learner is placed into an ongoing active batch starting from the exact module where they paused.

## 3. Extension of LMS Portal and Mentor Access
- Pausing a batch extends LMS access by the exact duration of the approved pause period.
- Access to 1-on-1 mentor sessions resumes automatically upon joining the new batch.
- Batch pauses do not incur any penalty or administrative charge for the first pause request. Subsequent pause requests (more than 2) incur a reactivation processing fee of ₹3,500.
