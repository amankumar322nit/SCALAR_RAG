# Scaler Academy: Software Development Program

## 1. Program Overview
Scaler Academy is an intensive, 9-to-11 month software engineering program designed to transform software developers and tech enthusiasts into high-performing SDE-2 and SDE-3 engineers. The program includes live interactive classes, structured homework, real-world industry capstone projects, and 1-on-1 mentorship.

## 2. Core Curriculum Modules
- **Module 1: Programming Constructs & Problem Solving**: Time and space complexity analysis, recursion, bit manipulation, arrays, and basic strings.
- **Module 2: Advanced Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA)**: Trees, Binary Search Trees, Tries, Heaps, Dynamic Programming (1D & 2D), Graphs, Greedy Algorithms, and Backtracking.
- **Module 3: Low-Level Design (LLD) & Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)**: Design patterns (Singleton, Factory, Strategy, Observer, Decorator), SOLID principles, UML diagrams, schema design, and concurrency in Java/C++/Python.
- **Module 4: High-Level System Design (HLD)**: Scalability fundamentals, load balancing, caching strategies (Redis, Memcached), database sharding & replication, message queues (Kafka, RabbitMQ), Microservices vs Monoliths, Rate limiters, URL shortener design, and distributed tracing.
- **Module 5: Full Stack Web Specialization (Elective)**: React.js, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, RESTful APIs, WebSockets, Docker, and CI/CD pipelines.

## 3. Class Schedule and Time Commitment
- **Class Timings**: Live classes are conducted 3 days a week on Tuesday, Thursday (8:00 PM to 10:30 PM IST), and Sunday (10:00 AM to 1:00 PM IST).
- **Time Commitment**: Learners are recommended to dedicate 12 to 15 hours per week including live classes, practice problems, and assignments.
- **Recordings**: High-definition video recordings of all live sessions are uploaded to the LMS within 4 hours of class completion and remain accessible with lifetime validity.

## 4. Mentorship and Doubt Resolution
- **1-on-1 Mentorship**: Every learner is paired with an industry mentor from Tier-1 tech companies for bi-weekly 45-minute guidance sessions.
- **Teaching Assistants (TAs)**: Live doubt support is available on the Scaler portal between 10:00 AM and 12:00 AM midnight IST with an average response time under 15 minutes.
