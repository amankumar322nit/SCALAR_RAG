# Scaler Payments, Invoicing, and Tax FAQs

## 1. GST Invoices and Corporate Reimbursement
- **Q: Can I obtain a GST invoice in my company's name for corporate reimbursement?**
  **A:** Yes. During checkout or within 3 business days of fee payment, provide your company's registered legal name and 15-digit GSTIN under "Billing Details" on the payment gateway or email finance@scaler.com. Scaler will issue a B2B tax invoice enabling input tax credit.
- **Q: Does Scaler support direct corporate sponsorship or employer purchase orders?**
  **A:** Yes. Scaler's enterprise desk assists corporate L&D teams with direct vendor agreements, PO-based invoicing, and bulk enrollment discounts.

## 2. Payment Modes and Currency
- **Q: What payment options are accepted for enrollment?**
  **A:** Scaler accepts all major Indian and International Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), Net Banking across 50+ banks, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and zero-cost NBFC EMIs.
- **Q: Are international currency payments supported?**
  **A:** Yes. International learners can pay via international credit cards (USD, EUR, GBP, SGD, AED) or through direct international wire transfer / SWIFT.

## 3. Late Payment and EMI Due Dates
- **Q: What happens if an EMI payment is delayed?**
  **A:** EMI auto-debit dates are set on either the 5th or 10th of every month depending on the lending NBFC. A 3-day grace period is provided. Beyond the grace period, standard lender late fees and credit bureau reporting may apply.
