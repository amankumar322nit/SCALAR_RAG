# Scaler Learner Support: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. LMS and Class Recordings
- **Q: What happens if I miss a live lecture?**
  **A:** All live sessions are recorded in full HD and uploaded to your Learner Dashboard within 4 hours. You can watch missed sessions anytime, replay at multiple playback speeds (1x to 2x), and read the accompanying class notes.
- **Q: How long do I retain access to class recordings and study materials?**
  **A:** Learners receive lifetime access to lecture recordings, assignments, lecture notes, and solution repositories even after course graduation.

## 2. Mentorship and Doubt Solving
- **Q: How do I book a 1-on-1 session with my mentor?**
  **A:** Mentorship sessions can be scheduled directly via the "Mentorship" tab on the LMS. Learners are allocated 2 mentor credits per month. Unused credits rollover up to a maximum of 4 credits.
- **Q: What are the Teaching Assistant (TA) doubt support hours?**
  **A:** Dedicated TAs are available daily from 10:00 AM to 12:00 AM midnight IST for chat and screen-share debugging. Average wait time is under 15 minutes.

## 3. Certificates and Graduation
- **Q: What are the criteria to receive the Scaler Completion Certificate?**
  **A:** To earn the official verified graduation certificate, learners must maintain >= 80% attendance in live lectures and achieve >= 75% score across all module capstone assessments.
